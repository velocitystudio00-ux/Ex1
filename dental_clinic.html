FAQ

Answers to common dental care questions

Are root canal treatments painful?

Not at all. With modern local anesthesia and advanced rotary techniques, root canal therapy is generally no more uncomfortable than getting a standard filling.

How often should I visit the dentist?

We recommend scheduling a routine checkup and cleaning every 6 months to prevent plaque buildup and catch issues early.

Do you treat children?

Yes, we offer specialized pediatric dental care in a friendly and welcoming atmosphere designed to keep young patients relaxed.

What payment options do you accept?

We accept cash, credit/debit cards, UPI payments, and offer flexible installment options for major treatments like implants.
